Clipbot Privacy Policy

Last updated: /11/15/26/

What Clipbot is

Clipbot is software that runs on the operator's own computer. It is operated by Aiden Smith and is not a hosted service. It creates short video clips from live streams of streamers who have given permission, and uploads approved clips to the operator's YouTube channel.

Data Clipbot accesses

When you sign in with Google, Clipbot asks for permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope). It uses this permission only to upload clips that you approve. It does not read your videos, comments, subscribers, analytics, or any other account data.

How data is stored and shared

The sign-in token is stored only in a file on your own computer. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and we do not collect, sell, or share Google user data. Clips, transcripts, and settings are also stored only on your computer.

YouTube and Google

Clipbot uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can remove Clipbot's access at any time at Google security settings, and delete the token file from the program folder.
